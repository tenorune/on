P1Two presence contracts
"Direct: you are visible to the people you handed your key. Room: joining is conscious mutual exposure under a separately chosen identity."

Direct presence flows toward those who chose you. A room is a deliberate second contract — less intimate, differently consented, and lived under a per-room name and per-room status that override Direct independently.

changedYour "second contract / conscious identity" guidance folded in; the v1 strain (group exposure) is now the contract itself, not a violation.

RULING
P2Possession of the key is consent; asking is requesting the key
"Holding someone's code or link is the grant. Request-to-follow is how you ask for a key you don't hold."

One consent model, two acquisition paths. And a derivation now made explicit: following someone necessarily hands them your key — the act writes your code into their world — which is why follow-back is automatic and legitimate: they gave you the key when they followed.

changedYour reframe dissolves v1's "two consent philosophies" tension entirely. The circled-plus icon collision becomes a pure vocabulary problem.

RULING
P3You pick the name you're introduced by
"Every first-contact name is chosen by the person it describes. After that, what people call you is their private business — and nobody can ever rename you."

First-contact names: the invite label, the room display name, the redeemer's published name. Post-contact: labels are viewer-private and invisible to their subject; even notifications speak the viewer's private vocabulary.

changedJargon removed ("doorway" is gone); ruled A in pass 1, restated for confirmation.

RULING
P4Your consent actions are legible to you first
"Accepting, declining, joining, leaving — the actor can always see what they did and where it stands. Silence to others, clarity to self."

Legibility to the other party is secondary; silence toward them remains the default. Phase 2 will judge, e.g., the immortal "Requested" button against the self-legibility clause, not against notification of others.

changedAmended per your ruling — this replaces v1's blanket "departures are silent."

RULING
P5Only warmth interrupts
"A push means presence or an invitation — never graph bookkeeping."

Knock, call, availability (opt-in per person); invite, follow-request (directed asks, above the opt-in regime as inherently consensual). Edges forming or dissolving never notify anyone.

unchangedKept as-is; boundary confirmed in pass 1.
