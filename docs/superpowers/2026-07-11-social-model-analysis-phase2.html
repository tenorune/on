The target model, in one page

The grand ruleset
  1. The key rule (P2): every relationship begins with a key. Holding it is standing consent. Following hands your key back — every follow seeds a possible mutuality (P6). Where you see someone and hold no key (rooms only), you may ask.
  2. Two contracts (P1): Direct is key-based and ladder-governed (watch < knock < call, P8). A room is curated mutual exposure under a chosen identity (P7).
  3. Curation (P7): the curator admits, expels, and can hand off. The room outlives its founder.
  4. Naming (P3): you pick the name you're introduced by, everywhere; afterwards names are private property of their holders; nobody can rename you.
  5. Legibility of self (P4): every consent act you take has a truthful, eventually-resolving state. Silence to others stays.
  6. Interrupts (P5): unchanged — presence and invitations only.
  7. The founding act (P2/R1): key-sharing gets a first-class in-app surface, always carrying your name.
  8. Surfaces: capabilities stay identical everywhere; formation/management converge (#286, #290 adjacent).
